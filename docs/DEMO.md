# Demo script (3 minutes)

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem and audience
“Before a software agency says yes to a small request, someone needs to understand what changes. DeltaProof connects a client request to the agreement and to dependent work. Everything you see here is synthetic example data.”

Show the seeded review workspace. Explain no account or API key is needed.

## 0:25–1:05 — Real AI, visible uncertainty
Click Trace this change. The local MiniLM worker embeds the requests and scope clauses.

“The spreadsheet request is related to both a PDF invoice inclusion and a bulk export exclusion. DeltaProof exposes that conflict. It does not decide for us.”

Read SOW-04. Propose a change, enter 12h, select Order data layer, add a note. For team invites, review SOW-05 and enter 20h against Identity & access. Review the color clause and mark included. Explain that the model abstained on this paraphrase.

## 1:05–1:45 — Impact and tradeoff
Open Impact simulator. Explain 32h and $2,880 are reviewer estimates and deterministic arithmetic. The two affected workstreams run in parallel under this simplified model, producing one working day of delay after buffer.

Defer invitations. Effort drops to 12h and the proposal to $1,080. Schedule delay remains one day because the export path is still critical. This distinction demonstrates why effort and delay are different.

## 1:45–2:15 — Challenge the AI evidence
Hide SOW-05. The team-invitation recommendation becomes insufficient evidence. Explain that the original review remains intact. This is sensitivity within the retrieved evidence set, not a full retraining or retrieval rerun.

## 2:15–2:45 — Complete the workflow
Open Review packet. Show exact sources, human notes, assumptions and input hash. Export Markdown. This is an internal draft, with no automatic client communication or approval.

## 2:45–3:00 — Technical credibility and limits
“Real browser inference, deterministic tests, reproducible evaluation. Ten of twelve synthetic smoke cases matched the expected source or abstention. That is a development check, not proof of contract accuracy. Next we will test with agency reviewers.”
