Checked September 8, 2026 IST. Official event: https://ai-builders-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/

## Deliverables and scoring

The overview requests a functional AI product, public GitHub source with setup documentation, a video no longer than five minutes, and a deck no longer than ten slides. The deck covers problem, solution, audience, features, architecture, AI, value and roadmap. Submit team information and required links.

Weights: originality 20%, implementation 25%, impact 25%, UX 15%, presentation 15%. No mandatory model, API or sponsor integration appears in the reviewed event materials.

Overview prizes: Best SaaS Product, $4,000 for one winner; NexFellow Founder’s Choice, Product Excellence and Innovation, respectively three, two and one months of Founder Plan; Tin Computer, $299 credits each for the first 100 eligible teams, one claim per team, subject to availability and a 60-day claim window. The headline $33,900 mixes cash with credits. The rules page still says prizes TBD. Do not represent all prizes as unrestricted cash.

## Eligibility and timing

The rules allow individuals or teams, including developers, researchers, founders, students and recent graduates. Work must originate during the hackathon and address AI/intelligent systems. The overview also lists age of majority, geographic exclusions and exclusion of companies/professional organizations. A manager explicitly clarified that “Students only” is a display error. Personal age/residency eligibility and any team limit still require checking before submission. The exact excluded-territory list and maximum team size are not stated on the reviewed rules page.
